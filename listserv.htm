Computer Science ListServ

The ListServ is an email mailing list for Computer Science students and graduates. Subscribers receive information about potential CWE positions, jobs, and changes here at CCC.

Who can join?

Anybody enrolled in a Computer Science degree program, or anybody who has graduated with a Computer Science degree.

How to subscribe

Send an email to cstechs@streetercs.net.

Put subscribe in the subject line. Leave the body blank.

How to unsubscribe

Send an email to cstechs@streetercs.net.

Put unsubscribe in the subject line. Leave the body blank.

Use the email address you want subscribed to the list. To unsubscribe, send from the address you subscribed with.

Having problems?

If you have problems signing up, contact Eric McBride.
